mindmeld

modulo

A skinnable step sequencer with a command prompt. Lives at modulo.amplib.app. source ↗

avva

One machine turns music into color, the other color into music. Point them at each other, and then get in-between. Lives at avva.amplib.app — it has a relay server and a controller app, so it is not a static build like the others. source ↗

Stegassettes

A cartridge is a PNG with something hidden in its pixels. Reading it back develops the picture again. All of it is built on @amplib/steganography and lives on stega.now.

stega.now

The player. Add a cartridge and it decodes in the browser and plays back whatever it holds — audio, an image, text, or a whole HTML app — while the picture develops out of its own pixels.

make

The editor. Drop in an image and something to hide in it, choose how the two combine, and get a cartridge back.

me

A cartridge of you. Record yourself, lay the frames out in a template, and hide the recording’s own audio in the picture it made.

audio-console

Albums built out of cartridges. A song is split across one or more images per track, with a cover cartridge holding the metadata, ownership, timestamped lyrics, and the key that decrypts the tracks.

live

An homage to performances that have made me feel alive. Screenshots of videos with the sound hidden in their pixels.
