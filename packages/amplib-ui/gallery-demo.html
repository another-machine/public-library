the exhibit language — the room a finished work hangs in.

every image on this page is painted by the page itself, so nothing loads from disk. select a work and it decodes, then comes to light. scroll for the next room.

this room sets --gal-backdrop to its own work, enlarged and veiled behind it.

no --gal-backdrop here — the veil sits over the page's own background, so every room shares one wall.

a rail rests at --gal-dim-deep, so the work under attention is the one that reads.

the vocabulary

.gallery a deck of rooms, snapped — vertical, or a promenade with data-axis="x". .room one stop, its work centred. .placard an intro or colophon. .work the chromeless toggle a piece hangs in; state is aria-pressed. .rail a strip of works sharing one wall. .gal-ghost withdrawn but still holding its place. .gal-glow the halo a work casts. data-loading a room decoding — its stand-in breathes.

attention is brightness, never colour: a work rests dimmed and comes to full light under hover or selection. the works themselves are in colour — the rule is about the room, not the art.
